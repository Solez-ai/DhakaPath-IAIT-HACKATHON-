About this data

Demo data, clearly labelled. Hub coordinates, link distances, fares, headways, flood vulnerabilities and the seeded street reports in DhakaPath are realistic estimates assembled for this prototype. They are not official BRTA, DMTCL or DNCC figures, and they are not a live feed.

What is real: the weather comes from Open-Meteo for Dhaka when the server is running, and the map tiles come from OpenStreetMap.

Every number is computed, not written. Routes come from the link graph, fares from the fare rules, on-time odds from a seeded Monte-Carlo run. Ask the engine — the admin panel will show you the same tables the math reads from.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are fares calculated?

Fares are computed per mode from the fare rules table (per-km rate, minimum fare, student discount, surge multipliers), not typed in by hand. The rates are realistic estimates for this prototype, not official BRTA or DMTCL tariffs. The Admin tab shows the exact table the math reads from.

Does student pass apply to all modes?

The 50% student discount with a ৳10 floor applies to public buses. MRT-6, leguna, rickshaw, CNG and ferry links do not carry a student rate in this model, and the fare ledger says so on every leg. The rules live in one table: change it in the Admin tab and every screen follows.

How does location detection work?

We use your browser's HTML5 Geolocation API with strict privacy protection. Coordinates are used solely for localized route planning and crowd reporting.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Minimization: DhakaPath does not require registration or store personal identity markers.

2. Geolocation: Device GPS coordinates are processed strictly client-side to calculate local transit distances and are never sold to third parties.

3. Crowd Reports: Incident submissions (waterlogging, resting spots) are stored anonymously to inform the city transit graph.
